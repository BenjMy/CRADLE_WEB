<p class="lead">
Sharing what SPAC-CRADLE learns with the scientific community, students, farmers and the wider public.
</p>

Aims at communicating and disseminating the gained knowledge across all communities, with outcomes aligned with Objective 4. Activities are distributed along the project's life, with a sufficient period allocated to writing scientific and technical articles.

<div class="callout">
<span class="callout-title">Key activities</span>
<ul>
<li>Scientific and technical articles published throughout the project</li>
<li>A dedicated three-month period at the project's end for knowledge transfer to students and farmers</li>
<li>Participation in dissemination events and on-site demonstrations</li>
<li>Outreach aimed at reaching the wider society-level audience</li>
</ul>
</div>
