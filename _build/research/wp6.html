Communication and Dissemination

Work Package 6

Sharing what SPAC-CRADLE learns with the scientific community, students, farmers and the wider public.

Aims at communicating and disseminating the gained knowledge across all communities, with outcomes aligned with Objective 4. Activities are distributed along the project’s life, with a sufficient period allocated to writing scientific and technical articles.

Key activities
  • Scientific and technical articles published throughout the project
  • A dedicated three-month period at the project's end for knowledge transfer to students and farmers
  • Participation in dissemination events and on-site demonstrations
  • Outreach aimed at reaching the wider society-level audience
