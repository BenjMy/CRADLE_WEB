SPAC agroecosystem management and societal impacts evaluation

Work Package 5

Applying the integrated WP4 model to real management decisions across three axes of agroecosystem stewardship.

Three axes of evaluation
  • Water and irrigation management
  • Agro-ecosystem function and diversity
  • Soil health

Concentrates on the application of the novel and integrated models developed in WP4 for evaluating relevant management considerations. A better understanding and modeling of the SPAC will enable new insights into these topics and highlight key processes and relationships to promote better agro-ecosystem management.
